## Quarter 2 Project Brainstorm

**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic we have covered was the idea of inverse design for materials. Inverse design in this context means specifying upfront how you want a certain material to behave, and then being told what structure the material needs to have in order to achieve it. It is well-studied how to go the other way (from material to behavior), but this inverse direction is where things get complicated. 

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate the relationship between the internal structure of a material to its behavior. This is a quite generic statement, but I am looking to explore it in the case of 2D-3D spring structures when hit by an impactor.

**What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

In the quarter 1 approach, our group is only 2 people and our current approach for splitting up work is too segmented. This means that we often decide one of us to do visualizations while the other does model training for example. I think that moving forward, it would be wise for us to work on similar things together so we can bring ideas and perspectives together to make more progress

**What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interesting in using techniques in model interpretation. Throughout my coursework there has been some emphasis here and there regarding interpreting model weights. In this project, the interpretation is very important and therefore I would like to research the field a bit more to see what tools I have at my disposal for the different models I use.
